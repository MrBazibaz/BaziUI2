BAZIUI ARTWORK V3 — SOURCE ASSETS
===================================
This pack includes actual individual RGBA PNGs, a gold BaziUI wordmark,
the original approved B emblem and banner border (copied unchanged),
and isolated visual elements from the new concept illustration.

IMPORTANT QUALITY NOTES:
- The original emblem and banner are preserved from v1.
- The wordmark is isolated from the concept, not typeset as a font.
- Concept-derived isolated assets are matte-extracted; inspect edges at
  100% and test in game before shipping. Some may need manual cleanup.
- The new button images are label-free and have metallic gradients,
  shading and fine surface variation. They are not pixel-identical to the
  concept illustration.
- Full scrollbar and progress examples are reference-size images;
  proper resizing requires slicing and/or separate game controls.
- Use 9-slice for borders/buttons rather than stretching corners.
- PNG support depends on the WoW Forever client; convert to BLP/TGA if needed.
- This is a graphics source package, not an installed addon.
- No changes have been pushed to GitHub.

Color identity: muted dark plum, warm bronze/gold.
Style: Vanilla soul. Modern comfort.
